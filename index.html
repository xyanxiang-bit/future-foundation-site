Our Work in Myanmar

Sponsorship that becomes a school, a calendar, a bicycle.

Myanmar has been Future Foundation's focus since our first school in 2011. These are three of the ways that support keeps reaching students today.

The Rebuild Myanmar Project

Inter Group was a major sponsor of the Rebuild Myanmar Project, organised by the Singapore Myanmar Chamber of Commerce and Industry (SMYCCI). Through this partnership and the support of fellow sponsors, a school-building initiative in Myanmar was recently completed — turning fundraising into a building students walk into every day.

A practical example of how sponsor contributions become lasting educational infrastructure.

Annual Myanmar Art Calendar

Each year, Future Foundation produces and distributes the Myanmar Art Calendar, featuring work by local artists. Corporate sponsors fund the print run — a S$500 package includes 50 calendars and logo placement on the calendar artwork — and proceeds go directly toward rural schools and classroom materials in Myanmar. Calendars are circulated to clients, partners, trade associations, banks, embassies, and airlines across the region.

Become a 2027 calendar sponsor

Changing Lives with Bicycles

On 3 August 2024, Future Foundation joined the 3rd batch of the "Changing Lives with Bicycles" programme, led by the Singapore Association of Myanmar. Bicycles were donated to students in remote areas so a long walk to school is no longer the reason a child falls behind.

Where It Started

Our first school, 2011.

Established in 2009, Future Foundation is the charitable arm of Inter Group, dedicated to education and social upliftment in underserved communities. In 2011, we built and handed over our first primary school in the remote village of Inn Ma, Ywar Lay, in the Irrawaddy Region of Myanmar — a place so remote that even cars could not reach it.
